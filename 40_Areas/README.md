# 40_Areas

An **Area** is a responsibility you hold indefinitely. It has a standard to maintain, not a finish line. Health, Career, Family, Finances, a business you run, a community you serve.

## Structure

```
40_Areas/
  Area - {Name}/
    COP - {Name}.md          <- required, one per area
    (any supporting notes)
```

Create a new area from `80_Templates/COP - Area.md`, then add a row to the Areas table in `10_Command Center/Vault Index.md`.

## What the COP does for an Area

The COP (Common Operating Picture) is one page that answers: what is this area for, what does healthy look like, what are the rhythms that keep it healthy, what are the numbers, and what needs deciding. The agent reads every COP at `/prime` and flags the ones that are overdue for review or showing red.

For an Area, the most important sections are **Mission**, **Desired Steady State**, and **Rhythms & Habits**. Those three tell the agent what "normal" is so it can notice when you drift.

## How many areas

Most people have 4 to 7. If you have more than 8, some of them are probably projects or sub-areas. Each area should map to one of the Roles in your Mission Document.
